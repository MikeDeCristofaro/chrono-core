# World 1 Room Layouts

## Room 01: Crash Site
- **Description**: The starting area where the ship crashed. Debris is scattered everywhere.
- **Platform Placement**: Flat ground with several elevated metal crates and ship wings.
- **Hazard Locations**: Exposed electrical wires from the ship and leaking coolant pools.

## Room 04: Rewind Well
- **Description**: A mystical chamber with a deep well that hums with Chrono Energy.
- **Platform Placement**: Spiraling walkways leading down the well. Central floating platform.
- **Hazard Locations**: Pulsing energy beams from the walls and a bottomless pit (requires rewind if fallen).
